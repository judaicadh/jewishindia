Sassoon Mills

Labels, ledgers, agreements, and photographs from the textile mills of E.D. Sassoon & Co. — the industrial backbone of Baghdadi Bombay. The collection here includes mill labels (David Sassoon, Rachel Sassoon, Elphinstone, E.D. Sassoon United Mills, Apollo Mills, and others), agreements, and family-related ephemera.
