Cemeteries of India's Jewish Communities

Survey of more than forty Jewish burial grounds across India — Bene Israel village cemeteries of the Konkan, the Baghdadi cemeteries of Bombay and Calcutta, and isolated single-grave sites at the edges of the diaspora. Many of these places are at risk; this map is built primarily on the field survey of Isaac Solomon.
